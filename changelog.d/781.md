- Normalize imported analytics and search evidence for scan joins (#781).
  `evidence-normalize` reads a supplied CSV/XLSX/JSON export or a saved
  `seohead.provider-evidence.v1` envelope fully offline and produces a
  versioned `seohead.normalized-evidence.v1` document against an optional
  `seohead.evidence-mapping.v1` manifest, keeping per-field provenance
  (envelope, declared, or unknown) for source, reporting identity, period,
  timezone, attribution, engine, collection coverage, and URL binding. A
  measured `0` stays zero while missing rows, null or blank cells, formula
  cells, suppressed values, uncollected periods, and failed or skipped
  collection stay explicitly unavailable. `evidence-join` joins normalized
  evidence to a scan, audit, or page list under the strict `external_join.v1`
  key policy with explicit relaxations only, preserves matched, crawl-only,
  external-only and unkeyable populations at their declared grain, and reports
  key collisions instead of multiplying rows; `--compare` plus a declared
  policy adds a pure compatible/incompatible/unknown decision across period,
  timezone boundary, identity, attribution, engine, and grain, and reports
  measured-zero quadrant eligibility for downstream cohort work. Restricted
  sources keep rows and identifiers inside a private local artifact and
  return counts only.
