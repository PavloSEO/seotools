- Project checklists can now record the agreed audit scope and report honest coverage
  ratios (#794). `project-checklist-init` / `seo_project_checklist_init` accept an optional
  `plan` — a reviewer, a URL `population` declared as a `complete_set`, a named
  `sample`, or `unknown`, with its `source`, `size` or enumerated `urls`, and optional
  per-`templates` populations, plus an optional `tasks` agreement (`all_agreed`, or a
  sourced `selection` of existing checklist item IDs) — bound to the project site
  identity and stamped with the write revision. Recording a plan upgrades the
  checklist to `seohead.coverage.v3` and appends to a retained plan history;
  re-recording an identical agreement is an idempotent no-op, a changed agreement
  stale-marks earlier evidence instead of re-evaluating it under a changed
  denominator, and single-plan v3 documents migrate on read. Reading,
  reconciling, or applying priorities never upgrades or invents a population,
  and v1/v2 documents keep loading unchanged. Items outside a selection are
  reported as `not_agreed`, visible outside every denominator.
- Checklist completion now follows the measured scope: a site-scoped automatic
  item backed by a limited (partial-scan) measurement stays unfinished instead of
  counting as complete, while an item scoped to explicit sample URLs still
  finishes when the artifact covers exactly that scope. The top-level `complete`
  flag now means the scoped audit is finished — every applicable agreed task
  done, no pending exclusions, and the agreed URL population fully covered by
  verified measurements — so unknown or unmeasured scope can never report a
  completed audit.
- Checklist status now returns `coverage`: five separate ratios — `audit_tasks`,
  `checks` (with `measured_urls`), `manual_review`, `deliverable_review`, and
  `url_population` — each with its own `basis`, `numerator`, `denominator` and `state`,
  so a URL count is never mixed with a check or task count. A denominator that cannot be
  justified stays `null` with `state: "unknown"` and a reason instead of becoming 0/0 or
  100%; measurements that cannot be verified against the agreed population keep the URL
  axis `partial` and count only verified eligible URLs. URL membership is verified
  against the agreed enumeration, so a population declared only by `size` counts no
  measured URLs and the numerator can never exceed its denominator. Per-template
  populations are validated as agreed sub-populations of the site population when a
  plan is recorded or read: enumerated template URLs must belong to the enumerated
  site set, and declared template membership can never exceed the agreed site size,
  so an incoherent plan is refused rather than trimmed or counted past the
  denominator. Saved check evidence
  now carries the measured URL enumeration, so the URL numerator is an auditable set
  rather than a row count. Multi-site aggregation sums the axes per site and degrades
  to `unknown` whenever a site cannot justify its denominator.
- A `not_applicable` record is now a reviewed exclusion: it requires a reason, a
  reviewer, and an inspectable evidence basis — a project-relative digested `artifact`
  or an explicit `evidence` reference. Reviewed exclusions keep their stable ID and
  history, leave the task denominators only through that recorded decision, and are
  listed separately under `exclusions`. A disabled item, a stale exclusion record, or a
  legacy `not_applicable` record written before the evidence-basis rule stays inside the
  denominator as `pending_exclusion` until reviewed again, so disabling, catalogue
  removal, or reclassification can never silently shrink agreed counts; a fresh
  reviewed exclusion on a catalogue-removed item resolves normally instead of
  staying pending forever.
