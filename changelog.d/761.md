- Add the software-review evidence pack for self-hosting, legal, and procurement
  review (#761). `scripts/generate_review_pack.py` generates and verifies a
  versioned dependency/license inventory (one profile per install group, with
  resolved versions and license sources measured from the generating
  environment, and marker-excluded, spec-unsatisfied, and uninstalled
  requirements recorded rather than dropped), a release-level provenance
  document tying the built distributions to a tag and the embedded build
  manifest's source revision, and a `SHA256SUMS.txt` covering all of it.
  Generation refuses a tag that disagrees with `pyproject.toml`; verification
  re-checks formats, recorded hashes, and re-extracts each distribution to
  re-validate its embedded manifest, the name/version its filename declares,
  and the `Name`/`Version` fields of its own `*.dist-info/METADATA` or
  `PKG-INFO` against `pyproject.toml` instead of trusting the record. A
  `SEOHEAD_REVIEW_CANARY_*` sentinel scan proves pack files and diagnostics
  carry no planted credential or client markers. CI regenerates and verifies
  the pack with a disposable tag on every run; the release workflow verifies it
  and attaches it to a real `v*` release.
