- `metrika-report` (`seo_metrika_report`) and `MetrikaClient.report()`: a Reporting API
  "Query is too complicated" refusal is now retried in calendar-month slices with a 5–15 s
  backoff; a slice that still refuses is resubmitted at a sampled `accuracy=0.1`. Only
  metrics provably additive over disjoint periods (`ym:s:visits`, `ym:s:pageviews`) are
  merged — a query for unique-visitor, percentage, average, or unverifiable metrics is
  refused before the first slice request rather than returning a summed value that would
  be wrong. Slices are merged as they stream in under one global row cap that also bounds
  downloads: once the cap is exhausted, remaining partitions are never requested and
  their date spans are named in `split.unfetched` — a slice stopped mid-way lists its
  own span there with `partial` — whole-period `total_rows`/`totals` stay unknown when
  the unqueried periods make them unknowable, and the returned window is marked
  partial. Merged answers re-apply the caller's `sort` (dimensions and metrics,
  or the API default of first metric descending) and take the `offset`/`limit` window
  from the global merged set. Sampling fields reflect what the API actually reported
  (`sampled`, worst `sample_share`), and a slice that does not report them leaves the
  whole-period value unknown — `null` in the CLI/MCP answer, never an invented `false`.
  `contains_sensitive_data` is the OR over slices, `min`/`max` are recomputed on merged
  rows, and missing or malformed metric cells and totals mark the body `incomplete`
  instead of reading as zero. The `split` block keeps the requested and resolved
  periods, the timezone used, and per-slice accuracy, attempts, dropped rows, and
  sampling metadata; a partition that fails names the completed spans and the
  accuracies exhausted. Relative dates (`today`, `NdaysAgo`) resolve in the request
  `timezone` or the counter's own zone (`time_zone_name`, else its minute offset) —
  never the host date. A period that cannot be mapped to dates (a shorthand such as
  `month`) is still retried and sampled before the error is re-raised. An explicit
  `accuracy` is honored in every documented form (`low`/`medium`/`high`/`full` or a
  numeric share such as `"1"`): anything above the last-resort sample keeps the
  `accuracy=0.1` fallback, while a caller's already-lower setting (`low` or a share
  at/below it) is preserved rather than resampled upward. Every attempted request —
  including the refused whole-range call — reaches the spend journal.
